Why I hike

Walking a trail gives me a chance to slow down, get some fresh air, and notice the world around me. I like that every route has something different to discover, from a quiet stretch of trees to a wide-open view.

Trail moments

Enjoying the view

Reaching a lookout after a steady climb always feels rewarding. I take a few minutes to rest, enjoy the view, and appreciate the quiet before heading back.

What I pack

For a comfortable day outside, I bring a few basics:

  • A refillable water bottle
  • A small snack
  • A light jacket
  • A trail map

Take only photos, leave only footprints.
